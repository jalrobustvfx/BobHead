A LITTLE CONTEXT

Big head. Same performance.

Upload a short video of one person talking, and generate. BobHead uses the first processed frame as a reference, scales head and face pose keypoints, then animates the scene and adds the original audio.

Clear, well-lit footage with one visible face works best. Head shape, facial details and motion are generated and may vary. The workflow uses a fixed enlarged-head pose guide.

Your clip is processed by the configured ComfyUI engine. In the default local setup, it stays on your computer. Uploaded files and job records stay in the app’s .data folder; engine inputs and outputs stay in ComfyUI.
